# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tech-savvy individuals who self-host — developers, privacy-conscious users, people who want control over their media consumption. They access the app primarily on mobile (iPhone/Android), browsing on the couch or during downtime. They are comfortable with the Instagram mental model but frustrated by the platform's dark patterns and noise.

## Product Purpose

Self-hosted web UI for browsing, rating, and archiving Instagram media downloaded locally via Instaloader or similar tools. A private, offline-capable alternative to Instagram's own app — same core loop (scroll, swipe, react), none of the platform overhead. Success looks like: open the app, see your media, act on it, close the app. No friction, no detours.

## Brand Personality

Sober, fast, reliable. The UI is a servant, not a performer. Content is the only thing that deserves attention.

## Anti-references

- **Instagram 2024+**: Overloaded, noisy, Reels-first. Dark patterns everywhere.
- **Plex / Jellyfin**: Feels like a media center product, too heavy for a photo browsing loop.
- **SaaS dashboard**: Sidebar navigation, stats cards, data tables — admin aesthetic.
- **Pinterest / Tumblr**: Masonry grids, decorative chrome, too expressive for a personal utility.

## Design Principles

1. **Content first** — media fills the frame; chrome is a whisper, not a shout.
2. **Zero friction** — interactions are immediate and predictable; no loading spinners for things that should be instant.
3. **Respect attention** — no algorithmic traps, no engagement bait, no dark patterns. The user decides when they're done.
4. **Functional honesty** — look like what you are: a tool. Earned simplicity, not decorative minimalism.
5. **Self-hosted pride** — built for people who value ownership and control; the aesthetic should feel deliberate, not templated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Correct contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, ARIA labels on interactive elements. No specific accommodations beyond standard practice.
